ARTICLE I PURPOSE 
The Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) is accepting proposals to establish 
contracts with qualified entities, including for-profit companies, nonprofit 
organizations, and private colleges and universities, to deliver instructor-led, 
short-duration training to Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) customers. These 
training services are designed to enhance vocational skills, support 
employment outcomes, and promote customer independence. Training 
durations typically range from one (1) to four (4) weeks but may extend up 
to six (6) months depending on the customer’s individualized employment 
plan. Procurement Services (PS) will administer the procurement process for 
this Solicitation, which includes Solicitation publication, handling of 
communications from vendors, as well as managing the receipt of Proposals 
for review and evaluation.  
Information regarding TWC and its programs is available online and can 
currently be accessed at www.twc.texas.gov. 
ARTICLE II SCOPE OF WORK 
2.1 OVERVIEW/BACKGROUND 
The TWC Vocational Rehabilitation Division (VRD) serves customers by 
referring them to training services designed to help individuals explore, 
develop, and attain skills that support competitive integrated employment or 
independent living. Referrals are based on each customer’s employment or 
education goals as outlined in their Individualized Plan for Employment (IPE) 
or Independent Living Plan (ILP). 
Respondent must affirm its intent to comply with the requirements as stated 
in Section 2.2 Specifications, and affirm that it will continue to comply with

2.2 SPECIFICATIONS 
2.2.1 Training Entity Responsibilities 
a. Providing instructor-led short duration training events for VR 
customers. Training solutions may fall into the categories of Pre-
Employment Transition Services (Pre-ETS), Blind, Visually Impaired 
and/or Deafblind Services, Neurodevelopmental or Autism services, 
Deaf and Hard of Hearing or an “other” category as described below. 
b. Providing training services that are designed to assist VR customers 
with disabilities to obtain knowledge and skills that prepare them to 
obtain, retain, or advance in competitive integrated employment or 
independent living, and may also prepare VR customers with the 
prerequisite knowledge, skills, and abilities to participate in 
postsecondary education, leading to competitive integrated 
employment. 
c. Utilizing instructors who are knowledgeable and experienced in the 
subject matter contained in the curriculum, are appropriately degreed, 
licensed, certified, or otherwise qualified in the areas pertinent to the 
training services and have experience working with individuals with the 
disabilities for which the training is designed. 
d. Utilizing curriculum and other training materials that are accessible 
and accommodating for the VR customer enrolled in the program. 
e. Providing VR customers with effective instructional and technical 
support whether the training is conducted in-person or virtually. 
f. Providing a primary point-of-contact (POC) for the contract who is 
knowledgeable about the contract requirements and training services.

[page 13]

g. Administering pre-event and post-event surveys to address the 
customer’s level of knowledge prior to the training and following the 
training, the customer’s satisfaction with the training, including any 
comments made by the customer regarding the training. The format 
and contents of the surveys will be mutually agreed to by TWC and the 
training entity prior to the initial training courses. 
h. Informing the VR customer’s assigned vocational rehabilitation 
counselor (VRC) or Older Individuals who are Blind (OIB) Service 
Specialist, within forty-eight (48) hours of any dispute that arises 
between the VR customer and the entity regarding the training 
services, and the steps the entity has taken or is planning to take to 
resolve the issue.
