ARTICLE I PURPOSE 
The Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) Purchasing Services (PS) section 
will administer the procurement process for this Solicitation, which includes 
Solicitation publication, handling of communications from Respondents, as 
well as managing the receipt of Responses for review and evaluation. 
Information regarding TWC and its programs is available online and can 
currently be accessed at www.twc.texas.gov. 
TWC is seeking to procure administration services for a Central Non-Profit 
Agency (CNA) to organize and administer the State Use Program, as outlined 
in Chapter 122 of the Texas Human Resources Code and Title 40 of the 
Texas Administrative Code (TAC), Chapter 806. 
ARTICLE II SCOPE OF WORK 
2.1 OVERVIEW/BACKGROUND 
a. The State Use Program, also known as Purchasing from People with 
Disabilities and Works Wonders, was established by the State of Texas 
to promote the independence of individuals with disabilities through 
meaningful employment, while at the same time providing state 
agencies and political subdivisions of the state with needed products 
and services at a fair market price. 
b. TWC’s mission is to provide leadership and direction for programs that 
empower people with disabilities to achieve maximum personal 
independence through productive employment. TWC operates under 
the philosophy that individuals with disabilities who desire employment 
should have equitable opportunities to participate in the workforce. 
c. The Successful Respondent will report directly to TWC. TWC oversees 
the State Use Program, which includes contracting with the Successful

[page 14]
RFP No. 3202600177 ver. 1 Page 14 of 74 
Respondent (a CNA) to manage the daily operations of the State Use 
Program and provide financial support, including oversight and 
recruitment of Community Rehabilitation Programs (CRPs). CRPs 
employ individuals with disabilities to produce products and services to 
sell to state and local government entities. The Successful Respondent 
will be responsible for recruiting and working with the CRPs, which can 
be more than one hundred (100) at a time. 
d. The 2025 Annual Report, submitted by the current CNA (WorkQuest), 
indicated that more than $82.9 million in annual wages were paid to 
people with disabilities, which represents approximately 6,137 people. 
e. As required by Section 122.009 of the Texas Human Resources Code, to 
the extent the records of the Successful Respondent (a CNA) pertain 
specifically to state purchases of the products and services of persons 
with disabilities, such records are considered to be open records for 
purposes of the Public Information Act, Chapter 552 of the Texas 
Government Code.

A written notice of Contract Award will be posted on the ESBD and sent to all 
Respondents immediately following execution of the Contract. 
6.2 CONTRACT TERM 
The Contract Term will commence on the fully executed Contract date and 
expire two (2) years after execution date. At the end of the initial Term, 
TWC will have the sole option to renew the term of the Contract for three (3) 
additional one-year (1-year) terms. 
The Contract is contingent upon the continued availability of funding. If 
funds become unavailable through lack of appropriations, legislative budget 
cuts, amendment of the General Appropriations Act, TWC consolidation of 
offices, or any other disruption of current appropriations, Termination 
provisions of Attachment 1- TWC Terms and Conditions shall apply. 
If the resulting Contract does not include a defined option period, the 
extension is limited to one year. 
6.3 PRICE ADJUSTMENT 
TWC approves the Management Fee Rate charged by the CNA for its services 
in accordance with § 122.019(e) of the Texas Human Resources Code. 
Currently, the Management Fee Rate charged by a CNA for its services is 
computed as a percentage of the selling price of the product or the contract 
price of a service; it must be included in the selling price or contract price 
and must be paid at the time of sale. The management fee rate must be 
reviewed on an annual basis in accordance with § 122.030 of the Texas 
Human Resources Code.

timelines below. Ongoing failure to meet these timelines and criteria may be 
considered a breach of contract. If TWC determines that any of the timelines 
and criteria are not met for any given calendar month, TWC will provide 
written notice to the Successful Respondent.

the Successful Respondent, or upon agreed-upon time frame.

within ten (10) Business Days from the day the report is 
requested. 
6.11.3  The Successful Respondent’s Account Manager must respond to

Business Day.
