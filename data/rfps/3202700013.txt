ARTICLE VI PURPOSE 
The Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) is seeking proposals to provide 
Conference Services in one or more of the following locations: 
1. Arlington, Texas;
2. Corpus Christi, Texas;
3. Conroe, Texas;
4. Dallas, Texas;
5. Denton, Texas;
6. Fort Worth, Texas;
7. Galveston, Texas;
8. Houston, Texas;
9. San Antonio, Texas; or
10. The Woodlands, Texas.
The Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) Procurement Services (PS) 
section will administer the procurement process for this Solicitation, which 
includes Solicitation publication, handling of communications from 
Respondents, as well as managing the receipt of Responses for review and 
evaluation. 
Information regarding TWC and its programs is available online and can 
currently be accessed at www.twc.texas.gov. 
TWC is procuring Conference Services, including meeting space, 
food/beverage services and sleeping rooms for the 2027 Apprenticeship 
Texas Conference (hereinafter referred to as the “Conference”).

ARTICLE VII SCOPE OF WORK 
2.1 OVERVIEW 
TWC’s Office of Apprenticeship supports apprenticeship programs that 
help employers build their current and future talent and workers progress 
in their careers and move into better-paying jobs. These programs 
support the creation and expansion of apprenticeships throughout the 
state. 
TWC has received United States Department of Labor (DOL) 
apprenticeship expansion funding with a period of performance through 
June 30, 2028. 
The Apprenticeship Texas Conference provides information to all the 
workforce partners that include, but are not limited to; Texas Higher 
Education Coordinating Board, Texas Education Agency, 
Community/Technical College, service centers, independent school 
districts, workforce boards, employers, etc. 
Respondent must affirm its intent to comply with the requirements as 
stated in Article II, Scope of Work, and affirm that it will continue to 
comply with these requirements during the term of the Contract issued as 
a result of this Solicitation, if any.
