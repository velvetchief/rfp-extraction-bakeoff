ARTICLE I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY, DEFINITIONS, AND AUTHORITY 
1.1 INTRODUCTION 
The State of Texas, by and through the Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC), 
on behalf of the Texas Department of State Health Services (DSHS), is issuing this Solicitation 
to solicit proposals from qualified vendors to provide Video Directly Observed Therapy (VDOT) 
services. 
VDOT enables DSHS staff to remotely observe Clients taking tuberculosis (TB) medications 
using video technology as an alternative to in-person Directly Observed Therapy (DOT). The 
VDOT solution must support both real-time video observation (Synchronous VDOT) and 
recorded video submission for later review (Asynchronous VDOT). 
The system must allow secure transmission of protected health information, support use on 
mobile devices, and reduce the need for routine travel associated with in-person observation 
while maintaining required treatment monitoring. VDOT will be used for eligible Clients in 
accordance with program policies and will supplement, but not replace, in-person DOT services. 
The use of VDOT decreases the amount of idle time spent driving to perform in-person DOT 
Services. Provider can travel up to six (6) hours per workday to provide medication 
administration to one (1) Client; with that interaction taking less than 30-minutes total. 
Providers’ knowledge and abilities are not fully utilized when they travel so extensively. VDOT 
allows the Provider the same interaction and observation for eligible Clients but decreases the 
need for repetitive travel. It also evokes a Client-centered delivery method to give the Client 
ownership and control over their treatment.  
HHSC Procurement and Contracting Services (PCS) will administer the procurement process for 
this Solicitation which includes Solicitation distribution, handling of communications from 
vendors, as well as managing the receipt of communications from vendors, as well as managing 
receipt of Responses for review and evaluation.  
Prior to award of the Contract, Respondent must obtain TX-RAMP Level 2 certification. TX-
RAMP Provisional Status does not satisfy the TX-RAMP Certification Level 2 requirement.  For 
this Solicitation, HHSC shall not provide “agency-sponsored” TX-RAMP Provisional Status 
certification to any Respondent. 
Information regarding DSHS and its programs is available online and can currently be accessed 
at https://www.dshs.texas.gov/.

ARTICLE II. SCOPE OF WORK 
2.1 DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES 
DOT for the provision of TB medications to Clients is the standard of care in Texas. The Centers 
for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), recommend DOT as the only documented evidence-
based intervention that ensures Clients are cured from TB using a supervised medication 
adherence regimen. VDOT uses technology to achieve direct observation, allowing Providers to 
allot more time to see more Clients. Implementing a VDOT program requires adherence to all 
current, approved DSHS Standing Delegation Orders (SDOs), Standing Medical Orders (SMOs) 
and must follow federal and state privacy and security laws. 
2.1.1 Texas Risk and Authorization Management Plan 
Commencing on the Effective Date and during the Contract Term, Contractor must
