1 Introduction and Purpose of IFB 
The Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC) is an agency within the Texas Health 
and Human Services (HHS) system. The Procurement and Contracting Services (PCS) division of 
HHSC administers IFBs for HHS. 
HHSC PCS is seeking competitive bids to establish a contract(s) for licensed off duty 
commissioned peace officer services for Region 6.  
Both HHS Agencies, HHSC and DSHS, will be entitled to use any contract awarded as a result of 
this IFB. 
To be considered for award, Bidders must submit a comprehensive Response which includes all 
required information and documentation as outlined in this IFB to ensure the Bidder meets all 
requirements, possesses the required experience and qualifications, and has the capacity to 
provide the services described in this IFB. See Appendix A, Submission Instructions and 
Response Checklist. 
1.1 Authority 
HHSC is soliciting the services stated in this IFB under Texas Gov’t Code § 2156.063. 
1.2 Historical Monetary Value  
Historical spend for the same or similar services is $809,666.68 for the period 09/01/2022 to 
08/31/2026. 
1.3 No Guarantee of Volume, Usage or Compensation 
HHSC does not guarantee any volume, usage, or compensation to be paid to any Contractor 
under any Contract resulting from this IFB. Additionally, all HHSC contracts are subject to 
appropriations, the availability of funds, and termination.  
The estimated historical spend included in Section 1.2 is provided only as a guideline for 
preparing the pricing response and should not be construed as representing anticipated or 
actual quantities that will be required.

4 HHSC Overview 
The Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC) was created in 1991 to oversee and 
coordinate the planning and delivery of health and human services in Texas. It is established 
pursuant to Chapter 523, Texas Government Code  and is responsible for oversight of Texas 
Health and Human Services agencies.  
PCS administers the procurement process, which include s IFB announcement and publication, 
Bidder communications, receipt of Responses, screening of timely received responses, and 
facilitating the evaluation of qualified Responses. PCS, in coordination with each HHS C program, 
manages the execution of the award, through issuance of Purchase Order(s) or other Contract 
document, resulting from this IFB.

7.1 Scope of Services to be Performed 
HHSC seeks bids from qualified entities to provide licensed off duty Commissioned Peace 
Officer services to protect State employees, agency clients, facilities, equipment, and supplies 
for HHSC facilities located in Region 6 counties: Austin, Brazoria, Colorado, Ft. Bend, Galveston, 
Harris, Liberty, Matagorda, Montgomery, Walker, Waller, and Wharton, as described below . 
The services shall be provided in accordance with the terms of this IFB, as necessary to meet 
the needs of HHSC, as determined by an authorized HHSC Security Coordinator  or HHSC 
Representative.  HHSC may, at its sole discretion, add or delete service locations as needed to 
meet its service needs. 
Contractor shall:  
Note: ONLY Commissioned Texas Peace Officers shall provide security services.  
A. Recruit and maintain a pool of Commissioned Peace Officers to deliver armed security 
services at all HHSC service locations located in Region 6. 
B. Schedule and notify Commissioned Peace Officers of duty assignments. Assignments 
shall be scheduled in a matter that ensures a Commissioned Peace Officer is on duty 
continuously during standard office hours of the assigned service location. 
C. Conduct weekly on-site supervisor or upper management visits to monitor 
Commissioned Peace Officer's performance. Performance is measured against SOW that 
is listed in section 7 and the site-specific Post Orders assignment given service manager 
or point of contact. The length of the site visit will be determined by the situation or 
circumstances. The contractor in coordination with the agency will determine the length 
of the site visit. 
D. Address performance issues and all other activities related to the effective and efficient 
management and administration of the Region 6 security SOW. The contractor will use 
the SOW and the post orders to address performance issues and all other activities 
related to effective and efficient management . 
E. Provide updates and/or other security related information with the HHSC Contract 
Manager and/or designee, as deemed necessary. Reports should be provided monthly 
to capture routine day-to-day activities.  However, non-routine/emergency incidents 
should be reported verbally within 2 -hours of the incident.  The contractor must 
provide a written statement/report of said incident within 24 hours . 
F. Manage all Commissioned Peace Officers at all services locations , at all times, including, 
but not limited to, ensuring Commissioned Peace Officers report for duty at assigned 
times and locations. 
G. In the event a Commissioned Peace Officer is unable to report for duty, ensure a 
qualified replacement Peace Office is provisioned to avoid an interruption in service.   
H. Provide a replacement Commissioned Peace Officer within two (2) hours after 
notification when an assigned Commissioned Peace Officer departs a service location

[page 13]

due to an emergency. The notification shall be to the Contract Manager from the 
contractor/and or HHSC onsite staff.  
I. Coordinate with HHSC for provision of services outside of standard office hours, 
weekends, and/or official State Holidays.  
Commissioned Peace Officer shall: 
A. Always be in full Commissioned Peace Officer uniform, neat in appearance, and have no 
visible impairment when present at an assigned service location.   
B. Remain at the designated security station located  in the service location lobby area and  
notify the local HHSC Office Coordinator and/or designee prior to leaving the post for 
any reason other than to respond to a security emergency or incident. 
C. Request any person who is continuously loud or boisterous at a service location to lower 
their volume or leave the premises. In the event of a conflict or disturbance, the Peace 
Office shall make his/her presence known and render assistance, as needed.  
D. Inform the service location management or HHSC Office Coordinator when there is 
cause to expel an individual from state property and cite the reason(s) for the action 
prior to its execution.   
E. Not occupy a private office at a service location or engage in lengthy personal 
conversations or other activities other than conducting continuous security services as 
specified herein.  Interactions with state employees and clients shall remain  
professional, brief, and necessary for performance of the security services. 
F. Not perform clerical, administrative, or non -security-related work such as setting up a 
conference room, moving boxes, or other logistical functions  other than security 
services. 
G. Become familiar with emergency phone numbers and service location evacuation plans . 
H. In the event of a security incident, complete a Security Incident Report provided by the 
Contractor and forward a copy to the HHSC Contract Manager within twenty -four (24) 
hours of the incident. The report shall be a comprehensive  and accurate account of the 
details related to the incident.  
I. Utilize Time Sheet Management System (TSMS) to submit hours worked at a service 
location.  HHSC will assign each Commissioned Peace Officer a unique ID that allows 
them to log in to TSMS.  Commissioned Peace Officers shall phone in from the service 
location to gain access to TSMS.  Commissioned Peace Officers shall not access TSMS on 
behalf of any other Commissioned Peace Officer.   
J. Refrain from the use of personal cell phone devices while on duty at service location.
